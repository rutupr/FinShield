# Product Requirements Document

## Product vision

FinShield helps banks, insurers, and customers make smarter, fairer, and more resilient decisions by analyzing loan and insurance information in a single workflow.

## Problem

Manual review of bank statements, salary slips, credit reports, loan applications, and insurance policies is slow, inconsistent, and prone to error. Binary decisions lack transparency and do not adequately account for future financial uncertainty.

## Core capabilities

- Collect applicant identity and financial details.
- Upload and validate financial documents.
- Extract structured data with NVIDIA AI.
- Assess income stability, repayment capability, fraud signals, insurance adequacy, and default probability.
- Generate an explainable Approve, Review, or Reject recommendation.
- Present a reviewer dashboard with a decision snapshot, financial summary, risk score, indicators, and recommendations.

## Success criteria

Recommendations are understandable, sensitive data is handled safely, exceptions are routed to human reviewers, and no automated outcome replaces final human approval.
