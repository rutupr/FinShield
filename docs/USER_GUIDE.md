# User Guide

1. Enter the applicant's full name, mobile number, and date of birth.
2. Provide the requested loan amount, monthly salary, and existing EMI obligations.
3. Upload the required income certificate, salary slip, bank statement, and credit report. Upload insurance information when available.
4. Confirm validation messages and submit the application for analysis.
5. Review the dashboard's decision snapshot, financial summary, overall risk score, income stability, insurance adequacy, fraud signal, default probability, and recommendation.
6. Read the explanation and supporting indicators before making a decision.
7. A qualified human reviewer makes the final approval, review, or rejection decision.

Fraud signals and missing or inconsistent information should be verified before any final action.
